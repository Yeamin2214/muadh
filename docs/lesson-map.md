# Mu'adh Lesson Map: 40 Lessons for the First Weeks

## The journey at a glance

Forty short lessons cover a new Muslim's first four to six weeks at one or two lessons a day. Each lesson has fixed sources and fixed prerequisites; the AI only picks which eligible lesson comes next for each person.

Stages, in order: Foundations (lessons 1 to 8), Purification (9 to 14), Prayer (15 to 28), Daily life (29 to 38), Wider practice (39 and 40). The order follows the hadith of Mu'adh ibn Jabal (Bukhari 1395, Muslim 19).

Prayer is the largest stage and the first practice after belief, so a learner reaches a first full prayer at lesson 21.

## Review process

Every lesson is reviewed by the team before it is published. Each Quran reference is checked on QuranEnc against the King Fahd Complex translations, for the right verse and for support of the lesson's point. Each hadith is checked on Dorar.net for its source, number and grade, and against the lesson's point. Each lesson is checked against the Scientific Package's four content levels: disputed points are marked Level C and referred to a mentor, and personal rulings (Level D) never appear in a lesson. Prerequisites are checked so that no lesson relies on something taught later.

## Rules every lesson card follows

Each card is short, sourced and safe to show to anyone, so the AI can order cards without ever writing religious content itself.

- **Length:** 120 to 200 words, readable in about 5 minutes with its audio.
- **Sources:** every claim points to a verse or hadith in the table. Quran and hadith text is shown word for word from QuranEnc and HadeethEnc, never retyped.
- **Hadith:** from Bukhari and Muslim, plus authentic hadith from the Sunan once verified on Dorar.net, as the Scientific Package allows, always with the grade shown.
- **Levels:** Level A and B content only. Anything Level C says "scholars differ, ask your mentor". Nothing Level D ever appears in a card.
- **Language:** simple words a new Muslim with basic English understands. Arabic terms are explained the first time, using the Al-Jamhara glossary.
- **Tone:** warm and encouraging, never harsh. One idea per card.
- **Duas:** only those found in the Quran or in the verified hadith, with Arabic, transliteration and meaning.
- **Numbering:** hadith numbers follow the numbering Dorar.net shows for Bukhari and Muslim.
